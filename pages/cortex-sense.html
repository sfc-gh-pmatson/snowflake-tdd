Cortex Sense
What it Is
Cortex Sense is the real-time runtime that gives CoCo and CoWork immediate business context, understanding of your business structure and operations from Day 1, with zero manual modeling.
Internal Index · Live 2.4M Concepts Indexed
How it Helps
It automatically builds and continuously refreshes understanding from the un-curated long tail of data, learning semantics and business processes across your entire data estate so CoCo and CoWork deliver increasingly accurate, relevant, and business-aware results as your data evolves.
