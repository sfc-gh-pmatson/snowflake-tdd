Data Mesh Organizational Listings
Organizational Listings
Package tables, views, apps, and AI models as versioned data products with metadata, targeting, and usage analytics — shareable across your Snowflake organization.
Listing vs Share
A share is the access mechanism. A listing is the product layer — title, description, discovery, versioning, and analytics.
Create an Org Listing
Wrap a share with a listing YAML — title, description, category, targets. One SQL command publishes it to your organization.
Targeting Accounts
Restrict visibility to specific accounts or business units, or open it to the entire Snowflake organization.
Consumer Experience
Consumers discover, install, and query listings via Snowsight — one click provisions live data access.
Listing Versioning
Publish v1 and v2 simultaneously. Consumers pin to a version and migrate when ready — no forced upgrades.
Provider Analytics
Track installs, active consumers, and query volume via DATA_SHARING_USAGE views.
