Generally Available
Zero-ETL Data Sharing
Share live data across accounts and clouds without pipelines, copies, or ETL. Consumers query your actual data — always fresh, always governed.
Direct Share
Share tables, views, and UDFs directly to another Snowflake account. Point-to-point, no Marketplace listing required.
Secure Views
Share derived, filtered, or joined views without exposing underlying tables. The view definition is hidden from consumers.
Listings
Publish data products to Snowflake Marketplace (public) or Internal Marketplace (org-wide) for self-service discovery and install.
Cross-Region Replication
Share data across clouds and regions automatically. Snowflake replicates; consumers query a regional replica at low latency.
Internal Marketplace
Publish data products for internal teams across your Snowflake organization — self-service discovery without central IT bottlenecks.
Governed Sharing
Apply row-level security and dynamic masking policies to shared objects — consumers see a governed subset of your data.
