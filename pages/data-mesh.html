Data as a Product
Package data as a product and publish it to the Internal Marketplace. Built-in access requests, usage tracking, documentation, and metadata make sharing and activation simple. Consumers discover trusted assets across clouds and regions.
Decentralized Ownership
Business domains own and govern their data products independently. Separate environments allow autonomy without isolation — no data movement required. Domain expert ownership drives higher quality and trust.
Federated Governance
Horizon Catalog gives domain owners global policies automatically: row-level access, column masking, data quality metrics, and lineage. One governance model across every domain — not bolted on, built in.
