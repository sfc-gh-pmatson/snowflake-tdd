Internal Marketplace
Self-service data product discovery across your Snowflake organization. Publish once; discover and install without tickets or ETL pipelines.
What is Internal Marketplace?
An intra-org data product store visible to all accounts in your organization. Providers publish once; consumers self-serve.
Publishing a Listing
Create a listing over a share targeting your org. It appears in every account Marketplace under "Your Organization."
Consumer Discovery
Browse, search by keyword or tag, and install data products in one click — no tickets, no waiting, no IT.
Access Request Workflow
Optionally require consumers to submit justification. Providers review and approve — all within Snowflake.
Usage Analytics
Track who installed your listing, active accounts, and query volume via DATA_SHARING_USAGE views.
Governance Controls
Masking and row access policies applied by provider are enforced for all consumers. Revoke anytime.
